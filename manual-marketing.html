Makhanani Means Joy. Cherished. Mercy.

Our marketing is not shouting. It is showing joy, showing we cherish schools, showing mercy through donations. Every post must show 1 of these 3: Joy, Cherished, or Mercy.

Headline: Makhanani LTSM — Scan Book → Scan Learner → Issue in 2 Seconds. No lost books. No paper registers.
Tagline: Makhanani — Joy. Cherished. Mercy. Working Together.
Target: Primary + High Schools in Gauteng, Limpopo, Mpumalanga first. Then nationwide.

4. What Never To Do
