👧🏾 Learner Manual — How To Use Your Book

Makhanani LTSM • Xintimani Group • Joy. Cherished. Mercy.

Your Learner ID: Example 10A001 = Class 10A, Learner 1. Your teacher will give you your ID. Keep it safe.

1. Getting Your Book

1. Your Teacher will call your name.
2. Check the book: Title, Barcode (e.g. WHS000001), Grade must match your Grade.
3. Sign the Loan Form - you promise to return it in good condition.
4. 1 Learner = 1 Title. You cannot take 2 copies of the same book.

2. Looking After Your Book

✅ Cover it. ✅ Keep it dry. ✅ Keep it in your bag.
⛔ Do NOT write in it. ⛔ Do NOT tear pages. ⛔ Do NOT swap with another learner - the barcode is linked to YOUR name.

3. Returning Your Book

1. Give it back to ANY teacher when teacher asks.
2. Teacher will scan it and mark it RETRIEVED.
3. Sign again on Loan Form: Returned Date + Condition: Good / Damaged / Lost.
4. If you lost it or damaged it, your parent / guardian must pay for it.

4. If You Lose Your Book

Tell your teacher SAME DAY. Do NOT hide it. Teacher will check Records to see which barcode you had.

5. Your Rules

• Return on due date.
• Bring book to class every day.
• If you move class, tell Librarian - your ID will change.

Remember: The book is NOT yours. It belongs to the school. Next learner needs it after you. Take care of it.
