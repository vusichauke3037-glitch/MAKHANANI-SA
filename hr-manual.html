Xintimani Staff Rules

  • Hours: Mon–Fri 08:00–16:30, Sat 09:00–12:00 (if needed)
  • Dress: Clean, neat, Xintimani logo where possible
  • Phone: Answer WhatsApp within 2 hours in work hours
  • Confidentiality: Never share a school’s learners, books, or passwords with another school
  • Money: No cash without receipt. All payments via EFT / approved method. Record every payment.
  • Respect: Respect principals, teachers, learners. No shouting, no rudeness.

Hiring (Simple Process)

  1. Director approves role + pay
  2. Interview (15 min) + practical test (e.g. issue a book in LTSM)
  3. Check ID + references
  4. Sign: Contract + Confidentiality + POPIA (see Privacy Manual)
  5. Training: 1 day — Training Manual + shadowing

Discipline (Fair Process)

  • Step 1: Verbal warning — private, clear, record date
  • Step 2: Written warning — what happened, what must change, by when
  • Step 3: Final written warning / hearing
  • Step 4: Dismissal only after fair hearing, with Director approval
  • Serious misconduct (theft, fraud, sharing learner data) = immediate suspension pending hearing

Leave

  • Annual leave: Request 7 days before, Director approves
  • Sick leave: Tell Director same morning + doctor note if 2+ days
  • Family responsibility: As per BCEA, tell Director ASAP
