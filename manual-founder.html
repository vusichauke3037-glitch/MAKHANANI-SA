Makhanani Means Joy. Cherished. Mercy.

In Xitsonga (Tsonga / Shangaan), Makhanani is the joy brought by a child, the feeling of something cherished and beloved, and the mercy of kindness.
Our Founder Promise: Joy when a learner gets their book. Cherished — every learner + every book valued. Mercy — donating FREE for 12 months to Wordsworth + George Sonto.

1. Vision, Mission, Values

Vision: Every South African school — no lost books, no lost time, no lost money.
Mission: Simple, affordable, offline-ready tools schools actually use.
Values: Ubuntu, Honesty, Excellence, Service, Community First. Joy, Cherished, Mercy in everything.

2. Founder Rules — Never Break

  • Decision Rule: If it does not help a school or make money to help more schools, don't do it.
  • Money Rule: No spending without recording in superadmin.html → Finance → Payments Log. No cash without receipt.
  • Pricing Rule: Never discount below locked pricing without BOTH directors agreeing. Demo Free 7 Days only, never free forever except donated schools (Wordsworth + George Sonto, 12 months only).
  • Donation Rule: Max 2 donated schools per year, 12 months only, must sign Acceptance, must provide contact person + learner/book list.
  • Hiring Rule: Hire for character first, skill second. 7-day trial, then decide.
  • Conflict Rule: Directors discuss privately, never in front of staff / schools. One voice to schools.
  • Privacy Rule: Never share learner data outside school, never post learner names/photos without school permission. POPIA always.
