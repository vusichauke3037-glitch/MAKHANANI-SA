🤝 About Xintimani Group

Xintimani Group Holdings (Pty) Ltd is a South African company based in Hillbrow, Johannesburg. We believe every learner deserves a book in their hands — tracked, accounted for, and cherished.

Our flagship project is Makhanani LTSM — a Learner Teacher Support Material system that helps schools manage textbooks from request to issue to return, with no duplicate, no skipping, and 1 Learner 1 Title.

🔄 How Makhanani LTSM Works

📝
1. Teacher Requests

Teacher picks own class + subject, requests books from HOD.

✅
2. HOD Approves

HOD of that department approves / rejects quantity.

📦
3. Librarian Packages

Librarian packs approved quantity for that teacher + class.

📤
4. Teacher Issues

Teacher scans Packed book + Learner in own class, issues.

↩️
5. Anyone Retrieves

Any staff can retrieve / return book. Becomes Available again.

Rules: Book WHS000001 • Learner 10A001 • No Duplicate • No Skipping • 1 Learner 1 Title • Grade Mismatch Blocked
