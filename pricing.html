❓ Frequently Asked

Do I pay Setup every year?

No. Setup is ONCE in lifetime. Year 2 onwards you only pay Monthly / Yearly.

What if I cancel and come back?

If you already paid Setup once, you never pay it again — we keep a record in Subscriptions as “Setup Paid”.

Yearly saves 2 months: Starter R5,000 vs R6,000 (12×R500), Standard R7,000 vs R8,400, Premium R9,000 vs R10,800.
