1. What Principal Can Do

Principal can View everything, Approve / Reject requests, Check subscription. Principal cannot Add / Delete books — Librarian does that. This protects stock from mistakes.

2. Login

Home → Login → Email + Password → Principal Dashboard for YOUR school only.
Wordsworth: EMIS 700310854 • George Sonto: EMIS 911360870. Check EMIS at top — never mix schools.

3. Dashboard — What Numbers Mean

Titles = Different book titles. Copies = Total physical books. Learners = Total learners. Out Now = Books currently Issued to learners.
Example: 50 Titles, 500 Copies, 300 Learners, 120 Out = 120 books with learners, 380 in library.

4. Stock By Subject / Grade

Dashboard shows Stock By Subject (e.g. Mathematics: 100 total, 80 available) and By Grade (Grade 10: 200 copies). Use this for LTSM ordering — if Grade 10 Mathematics has only 10 available for 100 learners, order more.

5. Approve / Reject Teacher Requests

Teacher requests book for class. Principal Dashboard → Requests → Tap ✅ Approve (ready to issue) or ❌ Reject (teacher must re-request with correct title / qty).
HOD also approves for their subjects. Either approval is enough.

6. Check Subscription

Bottom of Dashboard → Subscription: Package, Status, Start → End (12 Months Only), Setup Paid / Unpaid.
If Expired / Unpaid, school locks. Contact Super Admin: Mr Vusi Chauke, Finance Dashboard, to Pay / Renew.
Premium Yearly: R14,500 first 12 months (Setup R5,500 + R9,000). Renewal: R9,000 (Setup R0).

7. Book Number & Learner ID — For Checking

Book: WHS000001 = Wordsworth High School + 000001, No Dash, No Duplicate, No Skip.
George Sonto: GSHS000001
Learner: 10A001 = Grade 10 + Class 10A + 001, No Dash.
If you see a book with handwritten WHS-0001 or 10A-001, ask Librarian to re-print barcode from Print Barcodes.

8. What To Ask Librarian For

• Weekly: Stock List printout (Stock → Print)
• Monthly: Issue History — How many books out, how many overdue
• Term: List of learners with books not returned

Principal Rule: Do not share your login. Do not let learners log in as Principal. If you forget password, ask Super Admin to reset.
