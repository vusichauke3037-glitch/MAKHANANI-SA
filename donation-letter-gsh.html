RE: DONATION OF TEXTBOOKS, LIBRARY BOOKS & LTSM SUPPORT TO GEORGE SONTO HIGH SCHOOL (EMIS 911360870)

Xintimani Group Holdings (Pty) Ltd, through our flagship project Makhanani LTSM, is pleased to offer donation and support to George Sonto High School (EMIS 911360870), P.O. Box 1317, Malamulele, 0982.

We believe every learner deserves a book in their hands — tracked, accounted for, and cherished. Our donation aims to support teaching and learning, reduce textbook shortages, and strengthen your school library.

📚 What We Donate / Support:

  • Textbooks — Grade 8 to Grade 12, all Departments (Mathematics, Sciences, Languages, Commerce, Humanities, Technology)
  • Library books, workbooks, study guides and stationery
  • Makhanani LTSM system — FREE tracking: Request → HOD Approve → Librarian Package → Teacher Issue → Anyone Retrieve
  • Barcodes GSH000001 →, Learner IDs 10A001 →, Print A4 3×5 labels, Issue Record per Class
  • Training for Librarian, HODs and Teachers on stock control and retrieval

✅ How Donation Will Be Recorded:

1. Every donated book will be recorded in Makhanani LTSM with barcode GSH000001 →
2. School will receive a full Donation List — Title, Qty, Barcode Range
3. Librarian will organise stock by Dept → Grade → Subject → Title
4. Teachers request → HOD approves → Librarian packages → Teachers issue to own class
5. Any Teacher can retrieve books back to the library

This donation is made in good faith, with no cost to the school, and with the sole purpose of supporting learners. We kindly request that the school acknowledge receipt of the donation and allow our team to work with your Librarian and HODs to record and package the books.

We would be honoured to partner with George Sonto High School on an ongoing basis.
