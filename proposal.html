RE: PROPOSAL FOR MAKHANANI LTSM — TEXTBOOK TRACKING SYSTEM FOR SCHOOLS

1. Purpose

Makhanani LTSM assists schools to track, lend and recover the textbooks they already own. It is a digital management system that replaces lost paper registers.

Important: Makhanani LTSM is NOT a book donation programme. We do not supply books. We help schools manage the books they already have.

2. The Challenge In Schools

  • Paper loan registers are incomplete, damaged or lost
  • No follow-up on outstanding books at term end
  • Principals and HODs have no real-time view of stock
  • SGB and District reports are prepared manually and late
  • Schools lose money every year replacing books that were never returned

3. The Solution — Makhanani LTSM Workflow

A full audit trail from library shelf to learner and back:

01

📚 Librarian Organises Stock

Received, catalogued, shelved with barcodes

02

📋 Teacher Requests Stock

Via portal — subject, grade, quantity

03

✅ HOD Approves

Ensures curriculum alignment

04

📖 Teacher Issues to Learners

Recorded against learner profile

05

↩️ Learners Return Books

Checked-in, inspected, updated

Every Book Accounted For. Every Term.

4. What Schools Receive

  • Digital register by grade, subject and department
  • HOD dashboards — live stock, lending, returns
  • Learner borrowing history
  • End-of-term reports for SGB and District — in 1 click
  • Issue • Return • Scan • Print • HOD Approvals • Super Admin
  • Setup and training in 1 day — works on phone, no app needed, low data
