2. What Data We Collect: (a) School data: School name, EMIS, district, contact person, (b) Staff data: Name, role, dept, subject, phone, username, (c) Learner data: Name, Learner ID, grade, class, gender (if provided), (d) Book data: Title, subject, grade, barcode, status, (e) Issue data: Who borrowed what, when, returned or not. We do NOT collect ID numbers unless your school uploads them in a CSV — please avoid uploading ID numbers if possible.

3. Why We Collect It: Only to provide Makhanani LTSM: Issue / Return books, track overdue, HOD approvals, dashboards, DBE reports, support. We do not sell learner data. We do not use learner data for marketing.

4. How We Protect It (POPIA): (a) Each EMIS sees ONLY its own data — never mixed, (b) Passwords for Librarian, HOD, Teacher, Principal, Admin, Super Admin, (c) Learner names are hidden in DBE Dashboard — counts only, (d) We do not share school data with other schools, (e) Staff must sign Confidentiality + POPIA, (f) If you suspect a breach, tell us immediately on WhatsApp 078 023 3263.

5. Who Sees It: Only: Your school staff (by role), Xintimani support (to fix your issue), and DBE / District if your school chooses to share a report. No one else. We do not sell or rent data.

6. How Long We Keep It: While your school uses LTSM + 90 days after termination, so you can export it. After that, we may delete it. You can ask us to delete your school data at any time (see Section 8).

7. Cookies / Tracking: This system uses browser localStorage on your device to store your school’s data (so it works offline). It does not use advertising trackers. If you clear browser data, you may lose local data — always export CSV backups.

8. Your Rights (POPIA): You (school / parent / learner where applicable) have the right to: (a) Ask what data we hold for your school, (b) Ask us to correct wrong data, (c) Ask us to delete data, (d) Object to processing, (e) Lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator (South Africa) if you think we broke POPIA. To exercise these rights, WhatsApp 078 023 3263 or Email vusichauke3037@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

9. Children: Learners are children. We take extra care. Only school staff with a role (Librarian, Teacher, HOD, Principal) can see learner names. DBE Dashboard shows counts only, no names. Parents / guardians should contact the school first for learner queries.

10. Changes: We may update this Policy. We will post the new version here and tell schools on WhatsApp / Email.
