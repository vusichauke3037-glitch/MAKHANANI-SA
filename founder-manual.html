Our Story

Xintimani Group was founded to solve real problems in South African schools and communities. We saw books lost, no tracking, HODs with no data, principals with no reports. So we built Makhanani LTSM — a simple system any school can use: Scan Book → Scan Learner → Issue. No expensive software. No complicated training.
