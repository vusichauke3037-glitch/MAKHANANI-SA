Xintimani Group Holdings (Pty) Ltd is a proudly South African holding company based in Bushbuckridge, Mpumalanga. We were built to solve real problems — lost textbooks, manual registers, slow admin, and systems that don't work in rural schools.

📚 Makhanani LTSM — How It Works

Makhanani means “working together”. Teacher, HOD, Librarian and Super Admin in one simple flow — no lost books, no paper registers.

STEP 1Teacher Requests — “30× Maths Gr10 for Grade 10A”
STEP 2HOD Approves — Teacher can only issue up to approved number.
STEP 3Issue — Scan Book → Scan Learner ID OR Search Name → Issue.
STEP 4Return — Scan Book OR Search Learner → Tap Book → Return. Due 30 Nov.
STEP 5Super Admin — Creates schools, sets subscriptions, sees finance, HR, logistics.
