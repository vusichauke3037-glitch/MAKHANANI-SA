📚 Makhanani LTSM — How It Works

Makhanani means “working together”. Our LTSM system brings Teacher, HOD, Librarian and Super Admin together in one simple flow — no lost books, no paper registers.

STEP 1Teacher Requests — Teacher requests books from HOD: “30× Maths Gr10 for Grade 10A”
STEP 2HOD Approves — HOD approves / rejects. Teacher can only issue up to approved number.
STEP 3Issue — Scan Book WHS000001 → Scan Learner ID OR Search Name (2 letters) → Issue. 1 Learner = 1 Title, No Grade Mismatch.
STEP 4Return — Scan Book OR Search Learner → Tap Book → Return. Due 30 November every year.
STEP 5Super Admin — Creates schools, sets subscriptions, sees finance, HR, logistics for all schools.
